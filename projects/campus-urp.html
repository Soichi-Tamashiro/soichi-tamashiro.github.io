Context

Campus URP (formerly Yachayhuasi) is the core academic platform of Universidad Ricardo Palma — a Peruvian university of 15,000+ students, faculty, and administrative staff across 8 faculties. It runs curricula, courses, enrollment, lective and non-lective workload, schedules, classrooms, grading, and reporting across undergraduate, postgraduate, master's, specialties, and medical residency programs.

I joined as a backend analyst on the original build, and after launch was promoted to Technical Project Manager to lead the platform's consolidation and expansion. Its launch was covered by El Comercio, Peru's leading newspaper.

The challenge

The platform replaced a system that had been in service for more than two decades. The legacy stack was fragile and, in parts, only deployable on unsupported Windows versions. Two symptoms defined the pain:

  • Every academic cycle started in crisis. Launching a cycle required emergency committees — manual, cross-department firefighting just to get the term running.
  • Data was corrected by hand. Grades and academic records were routinely fixed with manual database interventions, with no audit trail and real compliance risk.

The mandate was not just to rebuild, but to make the platform governable, auditable, and continuously deliverable — under Peruvian data-protection law and national university accreditation (SUNEDU).

What I did

Owned requirements & architecture for the rework

I led the platform's complete rework end-to-end — from requirements and architecture through CI/CD, cloud (AWS/GCP) and on-premise deployment, to production support and accreditation.

Shipped the systems that removed the manual work

  • University-wide evaluations system — automated grade-validation rules, a three-level digital approval workflow, full audit trail and notifications. This eliminated the manual database corrections entirely.
  • Undergraduate-medicine hospital-program system — hybrid university/hospital schedules, rotations, supervising-physician allocation, and program-specific grading, integrated with legacy enrollment.
  • On-premise AI assistant — retrieval-augmented generation over locally-served LLMs, role-based authorization, and tamper-evident auditing, with no data leaving university infrastructure.

Built the team and the delivery engine

  • Hire, lead, and mentor a 10-person cross-functional team (backend, frontend, DBA/data, UI/UX, DevOps).
  • Introduced a per-project hybrid framework (Agile sprints, design thinking, waterfall gates) and coordinated CI/CD, releases, and the migration to a new datacenter.
  • Own hiring, app-store, integration, and AI/LLM budgets, defending cases before the university's budget office.

Made it governable

I lead the migration to a Keycloak-based IAM platform (owning role, permission, and resource design) and own technology governance and compliance — SUNEDU accreditation documentation, information-security and privacy-policy accreditations, and GDPR-aligned data protection with the university's Cybersecurity Committee.
