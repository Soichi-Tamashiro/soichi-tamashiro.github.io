Context

INSPIRA was a health-tech consulting engagement with biomedical research groups at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia (UPCH) and PUCP. The goal: give researchers a practical way to measure and track respiratory data, and to guide patient care around it.

The challenge

  • Turn a research need into a working system — hardware, mobile, and web — under a real clinical/research context.
  • Make the measurement device usable by non-engineers and keep readings in sync with a central panel.

What I did

  • Led a 3-person team delivering the full solution end-to-end.
  • Bluetooth measurement device — co-developed with biomedical-engineering students.
  • Mobile app for readings and patient-care guidance.
  • Web admin panel with data sync (PythonAnywhere) for the research team.

Impact

The system supported the researchers' publications and remains active — publicly featured by UPCH in 2025, years after the initial build.
