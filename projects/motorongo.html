Context

Motorongo is a green-mobility ride-hailing platform for the Peruvian Amazon — a two-sided marketplace connecting passengers and drivers that funds reforestation with each trip. It won Startup Perú 2023 (ProInnóvate, Ministry of Production) under its earlier name, Terabike.

Suggested: screenshots of the rider and driver apps from Google Play (public).

The challenge

  • Build a two-sided marketplace 0→1 — passengers and drivers, each with their own app and flows — for an underserved region.
  • Bake in the reforestation model so every trip contributes, without adding friction to the ride.
  • Do it lean, as a founding team, fast enough to win a national startup competition.

What I did

  • Co-founded the company and served as CTO.
  • Architected the backend, database, and Android applications — separate passenger and driver apps.
  • Built the product 0→1 and led 3 engineers.
  • Drove the winning submission to Startup Perú 2023.

Impact & recognition

Winner of Startup Perú 2023 (ProInnóvate, Ministry of Production). The passenger and driver apps are live on Google Play, with a business model that ties growth directly to reforestation in the Amazon.
