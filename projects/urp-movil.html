Context

URP Móvil is the university's official mobile app — used by students, faculty, and administrative staff for enrollment, schedules, and academic data, connected to the Campus URP platform.

Suggested: 2–3 real screenshots pulled straight from the store listings (fully public).

The challenge

Shipping the app is the easy half. The hard half is the institutional app-store presence — a public university publishing under its own legal identity has to clear administrative hurdles most product teams never see:

  • Obtaining the university's DUNS number (required for developer-account identity).
  • Securing Apple's institutional fee waiver.
  • Getting Huawei AppGallery accreditation (in progress).

What I did

  • Built and maintain the apps (Flutter) with a UI/UX designer and a full-stack engineer, connected to Campus URP.
  • Own the app-store presence end-to-end — obtained the university's DUNS number and Apple's institutional fee waiver; Huawei AppGallery accreditation in progress.
  • Direct the roadmap so the mobile experience stays in step with the academic platform behind it.
